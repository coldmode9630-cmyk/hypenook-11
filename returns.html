Return &
exchange

We want you to love what you wear. If something isn’t right, here’s how returns and exchanges work.

Exchanges

Wrong size? Contact us on WhatsApp within 7 days of receiving your order with your order number, and we’ll arrange a size exchange, subject to stock.

Condition

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition and packaging, with tags attached.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your item arrives damaged or you received the wrong product or size, message us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We’ll replace it at no extra cost.

Delivery charges

For size exchanges, the customer covers delivery charges. For damaged or incorrect items, HypeNook covers delivery.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved, it is made to the original online payment method, or by bank transfer or mobile wallet for Cash on Delivery orders.

How to start

WhatsApp us at 0336 8807765 with your order number and the reason for the return or exchange.
