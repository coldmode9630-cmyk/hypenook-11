Privacy
policy

This policy explains what information HypeNook collects when you shop with us and how we use it.

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, phone number, delivery address, city, province, and — if you choose to give them — your email and postal code. We also keep the items, sizes and payment method of your order.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your order.
  • To contact you about your order by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To handle exchanges and returns.

We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Online payments are made by bank transfer from your own banking app or mobile wallet. We only see the payment confirmation or screenshot you send us. HypeNook will never ask for your card number, PIN, OTP or passwords.

Sharing

We share your delivery details only with the courier delivering your order.

Your cart

Your bag is saved in your own browser (local storage) so it stays after a refresh. You can clear it at any time by removing the items.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by contacting us.
